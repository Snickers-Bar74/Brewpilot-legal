# Brewpilot terms of use

**Pre-launch draft, prepared 28 October 2026. These proposed terms have no
effective date and must be reviewed before launch.**

## Operator and scope

Brewpilot is operated by Jonathan Berentsen in Norway. Contact
[jonathan@nullmening.org](mailto:jonathan@nullmening.org). Brewpilot is a brewing
companion for recipes, calculations, reminders and optional AI assistance.
It is intended for adults of legal drinking age where they live.

## Brewing and AI assistance

Use your equipment's instructions and appropriate brewing, food hygiene and
pressure precautions. Calculations depend on the values you enter. AI-generated
recipes, photo readings and suggestions may be incorrect; check them before
acting. The app does not supervise equipment or replace safety instructions.

Do not submit other people's personal information or material you have no right
to use. Do not abuse the AI service, bypass rate limits or interfere with other
users' access. You keep rights to recipes and photos you provide; processing
for requested AI features is described in the privacy policy.

## Data, availability and software rights

Keep your own copies of important recipes and brew records. Notifications,
timers and online features depend on your device and external services. Features
may change or be unavailable; material changes should be communicated before
they affect users where required.

These service terms do not replace the Brewpilot source code's MIT license or
third-party licenses. No term removes mandatory consumer or privacy rights.
Norwegian law is proposed, subject to mandatory protections where you live.

## Contact and changes

Questions, corrections and privacy requests can be sent to the contact above.
An effective date and notice of material changes will accompany approved launch
terms. This draft does not announce a price, subscription or paid service.
