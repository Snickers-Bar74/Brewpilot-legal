# Brewpilot support

Email [jonathan@nullmening.org](mailto:jonathan@nullmening.org?subject=Brewpilot%20support)
with the subject **Brewpilot support**. Include your app version, Android version,
phone model, what happened and how to reproduce it. A screenshot is optional;
remove personal details first. Never send passwords, session tokens or API keys.

AI answers can be inaccurate. Verify recipe values and brewing instructions,
especially pressure, packaging, sanitization and equipment limits. Brew timers
and notifications depend on device settings; check a separate timer when timing
is safety-critical. If a reading looks wrong, stop and check the equipment and
recipe before continuing.
